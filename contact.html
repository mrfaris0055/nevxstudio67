Frequently asked questions

How do I choose my size?

Open the size chart on any product page and compare it with a shirt that fits you well. If you are between sizes, choose the bigger one.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes. Cash on Delivery is available across Pakistan. You can also pay by JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank transfer or card.

How do I place an order?

Add items to your cart, fill in your delivery details and tap Place order. Your order opens in WhatsApp and we confirm it with you.

How can I track my parcel?

Once your order is dispatched we send the tracking number on WhatsApp.

Shipping and returns

Delivery: 3 to 5 working days across Pakistan. Delivery is free above Rs. 5,000, otherwise Rs. 250.

Exchange: Wrong size or a defect? Message us within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unworn and unwashed with tags attached.

Refunds: If we cannot exchange an item, we refund it to your JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank account.
