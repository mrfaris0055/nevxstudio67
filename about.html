About Nevx

Nevx started with a simple idea: good everyday clothes should not shout. A man should be able to pull on a t-shirt or a shirt and know it fits, feels good and looks sharp.

We keep our range small and our colours calm: navy, ivory, silver and slate. Every piece is chosen for fabric, cut and how well it lasts.

We sell online across Pakistan with Cash on Delivery, so you can see what you get before you pay.
